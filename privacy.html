Privacy policy

OpenClaw is a personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not available to other users.

What it accesses

Only the owner's own Google account: Gmail messages (read), Gmail drafts (create), and Google Calendar events (read and edit), and only to carry out the owner's requests.

How data is handled

  • Data is processed on the owner's home server and, to answer requests, by the AI model provider the owner has configured.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties for any other purpose, or used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's server. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
